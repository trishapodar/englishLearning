Section B

Q5. Direct vs Indirect Elections
Marking Scheme / Steps: Meaning: Direct Elections - Citizens directly cast their votes to elect their representatives to public office. [0.5 Mark] | Indirect Elections - Citizens elect intermediate representatives (like MPs/MLAs), who in turn vote to elect the leader/representative. [0.5 Mark]
Example: Direct Elections - Lok Sabha / State Legislative Assembly (Vidhan Sabha) / Panchayat members. [0.5 Mark] | Indirect Elections - President of India / Vice President of India / Rajya Sabha members. [0.5 Mark]
Q6. Independence of the Election Commission
Marking Scheme / Steps: (Any two of the following points, 1 Mark each):
Security of Tenure: The Chief Election Commissioner (CEC) is appointed by the President but cannot be removed from office arbitrarily; removal requires a special majority in both Houses of Parliament on grounds of proved misbehaviour or incapacity (same procedure as a Supreme Court Judge). [1 Mark]
Service Conditions: The conditions of service and salary of the CEC cannot be varied to their disadvantage after appointment. [1 Mark]
Operational Control: During election periods, administrative and police officers on election duty work under the direct operational command and superintendence of the ECI, not the ruling government. [1 Mark]
Q7. Corrupt Electoral Practices
Marking Scheme / Steps: (Any two points from Representation of the People Act, 1951, 1 Mark each):
Bribery / Gratification: Offering money, gifts, liquor, or promises to persuade voters to vote, refrain from voting, or withdraw candidacy. [1 Mark]
Appealing to Religion/Caste: Seeking votes or threatening social boycotts based on religion, caste, community, or race, or using places of worship for election propaganda. [1 Mark]
Misuse of Official Machinery: Using government transport, state funds, or public resources for election campaigning by ruling party ministers. [1 Mark]
Proximity and Time Restrictions: Campaigning within 100 metres of a polling booth or holding public rallies within the 48-hour silence period before the close of poll. [1 Mark]

Section C

Q8. FPTP vs PR System
Marking Scheme / Steps: (i) Constituencies: FPTP - Country is divided into small, single-member constituencies; each elects one representative. [0.5 Mark] | PR - The entire country or large multi-member zones serve as a single constituency. [0.5 Mark]
(ii) Voter Choice: FPTP - Voter votes for a specific individual candidate. [0.5 Mark] | PR - Voter primarily votes for a political party list (or ranks candidates by preference). [0.5 Mark]
(iii) Vote Share vs Seat Share: FPTP - A party may win a disproportionately large share of seats with even a modest percentage of total votes. [0.5 Mark] | PR - Seats are allocated in close direct proportion to the percentage of total votes polled by each party. [0.5 Mark]
Q9. Inclusive measures by ECI
Marking Scheme / Steps: (Any three initiatives/technologies, 1 Mark each):
Braille-Enabled EVMs & Voter Slips: Electronic Voting Machines (EVMs) and voter information slips feature embossed Braille lettering alongside party symbols and candidate serial numbers to allow visually impaired voters to cast their vote independently and secretly. [1 Mark]
Home Voting Facility for Senior Citizens & PwDs: The ECI provides optional postal voting directly from home for electors aged 85+ and Persons with Disabilities (40%+ benchmark disability) with full videography and party agent presence. [1 Mark]
ETPBS (Electronically Transmitted Postal Ballot System): Enables armed forces personnel and government servants posted in remote borders or diplomatic missions abroad ("Service Voters") to receive postal ballots electronically and cast their franchise securely. [1 Mark]
Accessible Polling Stations: Mandatory provision of ramps, wheelchairs, transport assistance (via the Saksham app), and dedicated volunteers at every polling station. [1 Mark]

Section D

Q10. Elections and Electoral Commission
Marking Scheme / Steps: (i) Application Name: cVIGIL app (Citizen Vigilance app). [1 Mark]
(ii) State Party Recognition Criteria: A party must secure at least 6% of the total valid votes polled in the State Legislative Assembly election AND win at least 2 seats in the Legislative Assembly of that state (OR secure 3% of total assembly seats / minimum 3 seats, whichever is more). [1 Mark]
(iii) Why Money Power is a Threat:
It undermines equality by creating an unlevel playing field where wealthy candidates and corporate-funded parties outspend honest candidates with limited resources. [0.5 Mark]
It distorts free will through voter bribing and turns elected representatives into servants of private moneyed interests rather than the public interest. [0.5 Mark]
Remedial Measure: State funding of elections / strict public disclosure of political party donations / rigorous audit of campaign expenditures by independent ECI accounting teams. [1 Mark]

OR

(iii) Definition of Electoral Roll: An Electoral Roll (Voters' List) is the official, legally recognized register prepared by the Election Commission containing the names, photographs, and polling details of all citizens qualified to vote in a given constituency under Universal Adult Suffrage. [1 Mark]
Importance of Periodic Revision:
It ensures all newly eligible youth turning 18 years old are enrolled. [0.5 Mark]
It deletes the names of deceased electors or citizens who have permanently relocated, preventing fraudulent voting, impersonation, and bogus entries. [0.5 Mark]
