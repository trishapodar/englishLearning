Section D (Long Answer)

Q1.
If Planet-Alpha loses its atmosphere, the consequences would make the planet completely uninhabitable.

Extreme Temperature and Radiation: Without the atmosphere to trap the Sun's heat and regulate temperatures, Planet-Alpha would experience extreme temperature fluctuations—unbearably hot during the day and freezing cold at night. Additionally, without the atmospheric shield, harmful ultraviolet (UV) radiation from the Sun would directly strike the surface, destroying any potential life.

Collapse of Weather Systems: Since almost all weather phenomena like rainfall, condensation, fog, and clouds occur in the troposphere (the lowest layer containing water vapour and dust particles), the absence of this layer means there would be no wind, clouds, or precipitation. The water cycle would completely collapse.

Absence of Life-Sustaining Gases: The Earth's atmosphere contains a precise mixture of gases, including 78% Nitrogen, 21% Oxygen, and 0.04% Carbon Dioxide. Without oxygen, respiration would cease, and without carbon dioxide, plants could not perform photosynthesis. Thus, the biological cycle would end, leaving the planet entirely lifeless.
Marking Scheme / Steps: Impact on Temperature and Radiation (1.5 Marks): Explain that the atmosphere acts as a blanket regulating temperature (preventing extreme heat/cold) and shields the planet from harmful ultraviolet (UV) radiation. Award 1.5 marks for explaining both aspects clearly.
Impact on Weather and Troposphere (1.5 Marks): Explain that weather phenomena (rain, fog, clouds) are confined to the troposphere, which contains water vapour. Without it, there would be no weather or water cycle. Award 1.5 marks for linking the troposphere to weather systems.
Impact of Gaseous Composition (1.5 Marks): Mention the vital roles of gases like Oxygen (21%) for respiration and Carbon Dioxide (0.04%) for photosynthesis. Explain that their absence would make life impossible. Award 1.5 marks.
Presentation and Coherence (0.5 Mark): Award 0.5 mark for a well-structured, logical, and coherent explanation using appropriate geographical terminology.
