SECTION A: Objective & Assertion-Reasoning

Q1. (b) 2R
Marking Scheme / Steps: Step: Total time = 2 min 20 s = 140 seconds.
Number of completed rounds = 140 / 40 = 3.5 rounds.
After 3 full rounds, the athlete returns to the starting point.
After the extra 0.5 round, the athlete reaches the diametrically opposite point on the track.
Distance between opposite ends of a circular track of radius R = 2R.
Q2. (b) When the object moves along a straight line in one direction
Marking Scheme / Steps: Step: Average Speed = Total Distance / Time; Average Velocity = Total Displacement / Time. Distance equals Displacement only when moving in a straight line without reversing.
Q3. (c) Speed
Marking Scheme / Steps: Step: Slope of Distance-Time graph = (Change in Distance) / (Change in Time) = Speed.
Q4. (a) Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A.
Marking Scheme / Steps: Step: Velocity is a vector quantity (magnitude + direction). Continuous direction change in circular motion means continuous velocity change, which represents acceleration.

SECTION B: Short Answer Questions

Q5. Distance vs Displacement
Marking Scheme / Steps: Marking Rubric (1 Mark per valid point):
Distance: Total length of path traversed; Scalar quantity; Always positive.
Displacement: Shortest straight-line distance between initial and final points; Vector quantity; Can be positive, negative, or zero.
Q6. Train Acceleration and Distance
Marking Scheme / Steps: Conversions & Data: Initial velocity u = 0, Final velocity v = 72 km/h = 72 * (5/18) = 20 m/s, Time t = 5 * 60 = 300 seconds.
(i) Acceleration: a = (v - u) / t = (20 - 0) / 300 = 1/15 ≈ 0.067 m/s² [1 Mark]
(ii) Distance: s = ut + (1/2)at² = 0 + (1/2) * (1/15) * (300)² = (1/30) * 90000 = 3000 meters (or 3 km) [1 Mark]

SECTION C: Medium Answer / Graphical Questions

Q7. First Equation of Motion
Marking Scheme / Steps: Graph Diagram: Draw Velocity-Time graph showing initial velocity u at t = 0 (Point A) and final velocity v at time t (Point B). [1 Mark]
Derivation Steps: Acceleration a = Slope of line AB = (Change in velocity) / (Time taken) = (v - u) / t. [1 Mark]
Final Equation: a * t = v - u ⇒ v = u + at. [1 Mark]
Q8. Car Acceleration and Distance
Marking Scheme / Steps: (i) Conversion:
u = 18 * (5/18) = 5 m/s
v = 36 * (5/18) = 10 m/s [1 Mark]
(ii) Acceleration:
a = (v - u) / t = (10 - 5) / 5 = 1 m/s² [1 Mark]
(iii) Distance:
s = ut + (1/2)at² = (5 * 5) + (1/2) * (1) * (5)² = 25 + 12.5 = 37.5 meters [1 Mark]

SECTION D: Integrated Numerical & Graphical Analysis

Q9. Velocity-Time Graph Analysis
Marking Scheme / Steps: (i) Time Intervals (1.5 Marks - 0.5 Mark each):
(a) Uniform acceleration: t = 0 s to t = 10 s
(b) Zero acceleration: t = 10 s to t = 20 s
(c) Uniform retardation: t = 20 s to t = 30 s
(ii) Acceleration & Deceleration Calculation (1.5 Marks):
Acceleration a&sub1; = (20 - 0) / (10 - 0) = 2 m/s² (first 10s) [0.75 Mark]
Deceleration a&sub2; = (0 - 20) / (30 - 20) = -2 m/s² ⇒ Deceleration = 2 m/s² (last 10s) [0.75 Mark]
(iii) Total Distance Calculation (3 Marks):
Distance = Area of Trapezium = (1/2) * (Sum of parallel sides) * Height
Parallel side 1 (bottom base) = 30 s
Parallel side 2 (top base) = 10 s (from t = 10 to t = 20)
Height (peak velocity) = 20 m/s
Total Distance = (1/2) * (30 + 10) * 20 = (1/2) * 40 * 20 = 400 meters.
