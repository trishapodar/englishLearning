SECTION B: VERY SHORT ANSWER QUESTIONS (6 MARKS)

Q8. Motion Numericals
Model Answer & Marking (2 Marks):
Given: Initial velocity u = 0, acceleration a = 4 m/s², time t = 10 s
(i) Final Velocity [1 Mark]: Formula: v = u + at [0.5 Mark]. v = 0 + (4 × 10) = 40 m/s [0.5 Mark]
(ii) Total Distance Covered [1 Mark]: Formula: s = ut + (1/2)at² [0.5 Mark]. s = (0 × 10) + (1/2) × 4 × (10)² = 2 × 100 = 200 m [0.5 Mark]
Q9. Law of Conservation of Mass
Model Answer & Marking (2 Marks):
(i) Statement [1 Mark]: "Mass can neither be created nor destroyed in a chemical reaction." (Total mass of reactants = Total mass of products). [1 Mark]
(ii) Verification [1 Mark]: Mass of Reactants = Mass of sodium carbonate + Mass of ethanoic acid = 5.3 g + 6.0 g = 11.3 g [0.5 Mark]. Mass of Products = Mass of CO2 + Mass of water + Mass of sodium ethanoate = 2.2 g + 0.9 g + 8.2 g = 11.3 g [0.5 Mark]. Since Total Mass of Reactants (11.3 g) = Total Mass of Products (11.3 g), the law is verified.
Q10. Plant Meristematic Tissues
Model Answer & Marking (2 Marks):
(i) Apical Meristem Location & Function [1 Mark]: Location: Present at the growing tips of stems (shoots) and roots. [0.5 Mark]. Function: Increases the vertical length/height of the plant (primary growth). [0.5 Mark]
(ii) Differentiation [1 Mark]: The process is called Differentiation (where meristematic cells lose their capacity to divide and take up permanent structure and function). [1 Mark]

SECTION C: SHORT ANSWER QUESTIONS (9 MARKS)

Q11. Conservation of Momentum
Model Answer & Marking (3 Marks):
(i) Statement [1 Mark]: "In an isolated system (where no external unbalanced force acts), the total momentum of colliding or interacting bodies remains constant/conserved." [1 Mark]
(ii) Recoil Velocity Calculation [2 Marks]: Mass of bullet m1 = 20 g = 0.02 kg, Initial velocity u1 = 0, Final velocity v1 = 150 m/s. Mass of pistol m2 = 2 kg, Initial velocity u2 = 0, Recoil velocity = v2. Initial momentum = m1u1 + m2u2 = 0 [0.5 Mark]. Final momentum = m1v1 + m2v2 = (0.02 × 150) + (2 × v2) = 3 + 2v2 [0.5 Mark]. By conservation of momentum: Total Initial Momentum = Total Final Momentum. 0 = 3 + 2v2 => 2v2 = -3 => v2 = -1.5 m/s [0.5 Mark]. Conclusion: Recoil velocity is 1.5 m/s in the backward direction (negative sign indicates opposite direction). [0.5 Mark]
Q12. Bohr’s Model & Atomic Structure
Model Answer & Marking (3 Marks):
(i) Postulates of Bohr’s Model [2 Marks]: Only certain special orbits known as discrete orbits of electrons are allowed inside the atom. [1 Mark]. While revolving in discrete orbits, the electrons do not radiate energy. (Energy levels are designated as K, L, M, N... or n = 1, 2, 3, 4...). [1 Mark]
(ii) Configuration & Valency for Element X (Z = 13, A = 27) [1 Mark]: Electronic Configuration: K = 2, L = 8, M = 3 (Distribution: 2, 8, 3) [0.5 Mark]. Valency: Since it has 3 valence electrons in the outermost shell, it loses 3 electrons to attain a stable octet; therefore, Valency = 3. [0.5 Mark]
Q13. Cell Organelles & Osmosis
Model Answer & Marking (3 Marks):
(i) Suicide Bags [1 Mark]: Lysosomes contain powerful digestive enzymes. When cell metabolism is disturbed or the cell gets damaged, lysosomes may burst and their enzymes digest their own cell. [1 Mark]
(ii) Powerhouses & ATP [1 Mark]: Mitochondria release energy required for various chemical activities through cellular respiration in the form of ATP (Adenosine Triphosphate). [1 Mark]
(iii) Plasmolysis [1 Mark]: When a living plant cell loses water through exosmosis in a hypertonic solution, there is shrinkage or contraction of the cell contents away from the cell wall. This is called Plasmolysis. [1 Mark]

SECTION D: CASE STUDY & LONG ANSWER (8 MARKS)

Q14. Motion Case Study
Model Answer & Marking (4 Marks):
(i) Acceleration in Phase AB (0 to 10 s) [1 Mark]: u = 0, v = 20 m/s, t = 10 s. a = (v - u) / t = (20 - 0) / 10 = 2 m/s² [1 Mark]
(ii) Retardation in Phase CD (30 s to 40 s) [1 Mark]: u = 20 m/s, v = 0, t = 40 - 30 = 10 s. a = (0 - 20) / 10 = -2 m/s². Magnitude of Retardation (Deceleration) = 2 m/s² [1 Mark]
(iii) Total Distance from Area under Graph [2 Marks]: Total Distance s = Area of Trapezium OABCD [0.5 Mark]. Area = (1/2) × (Sum of parallel sides) × (Perpendicular height). Parallel sides: Base (t = 0 to 40 s) = 40 s; Top (t = 10 to 30 s) = 20 s. Height = 20 m/s [0.5 Mark]. s = (1/2) × (40 + 20) × 20. s = (1/2) × 60 × 20 = 600 metres [1 Mark]. (Alternative method by dividing into 2 triangles + 1 rectangle: 100 m + 400 m + 100 m = 600 m gets full 2 marks).
Q15. Long Answer
Option (A) [4 Marks]:
(i) Four Postulates of Dalton’s Atomic Theory [2 Marks] (0.5 Mark per valid postulate): All matter is made of very tiny particles called atoms. Atoms are indivisible particles, which cannot be created or destroyed in a chemical reaction. Atoms of a given element are identical in mass and chemical properties. Atoms of different elements have different masses and chemical properties. Atoms combine in the ratio of small whole numbers to form compounds. The relative number and kinds of atoms are constant in a given compound.
(ii) Postulate explaining Law of Definite Proportions [1 Mark]: "The relative number and kinds of atoms are constant in a given compound" (or "Atoms combine in the ratio of small whole numbers to form compounds"). [1 Mark]
(iii) Apical vs. Lateral Meristem [1 Mark]: Apical Meristem: Located at shoot/root tips; responsible for growth in length (primary vertical growth). [0.5 Mark]. Lateral Meristem: Located along the lateral sides/cambium; responsible for growth in girth/diameter (secondary lateral growth). [0.5 Mark]

Option (B) [4 Marks]:
(i) Newton’s Second Law & Derivation [2.5 Marks]: Statement: "The rate of change of momentum of an object is directly proportional to the applied unbalanced force and takes place in the direction in which the force acts." [1 Mark]. Mathematical Derivation: Initial momentum p1 = mu, Final momentum p2 = mv. Change in momentum = p2 - p1 = m(v - u) [0.5 Mark]. Rate of change of momentum = [m(v - u)] / t = ma (since a = (v - u)/t) [0.5 Mark]. By 2nd Law: Force F ∝ ma => F = k·ma (in SI units, k = 1) => F = ma [0.5 Mark]
(ii) Numerical Calculation [1.5 Marks]: Mass m = 5 kg, t = 2 s, u = 3 m/s, v = 7 m/s. Acceleration a = (v - u) / t = (7 - 3) / 2 = 4 / 2 = 2 m/s² [0.5 Mark]. Applied Force F = m × a = 5 kg × 2 m/s² = 10 N [1 Mark]
