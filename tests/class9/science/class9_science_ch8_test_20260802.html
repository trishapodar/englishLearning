SECTION B: Short Answer Questions

Q5. Bohr's Model Postulates
Marking Scheme / Steps: Marking Rubric (1 Mark per postulate):
1. Only certain special orbits called discrete orbits of electrons are allowed inside the atom.
2. While revolving in discrete orbits, electrons do not radiate energy.
Q6. Valency Calculation
Marking Scheme / Steps: Valency Definition: Combining capacity of an atom equal to the number of electrons lost, gained, or shared to achieve an octet configuration. [0.5 Mark]
(i) Aluminium (Z = 13): Configuration = 2, 8, 3. Loses 3 valence electrons ⇒ Valency = 3. [0.75 Mark]
(ii) Oxygen (Z = 8): Configuration = 2, 6. Gains 2 electrons (8 - 6 = 2) ⇒ Valency = 2. [0.75 Mark]

SECTION C: Medium Answer Questions

Q7. Rutherford's Scattering Experiment
Marking Scheme / Steps: Observation 1: Most α-particles passed straight through gold foil ⇒ Conclusion: Most space inside the atom is empty. [1 Mark]
Observation 2: Small fraction of α-particles deflected by small angles ⇒ Conclusion: Positive charge occupies very little volume inside the atom. [1 Mark]
Observation 3: 1 in 12,000 rebounded by 180° ⇒ Conclusion: Almost all mass and positive charge is concentrated in a tiny, dense central nucleus. [1 Mark]
Q8. Isotopes vs Isobars
Marking Scheme / Steps: Isotopes vs Isobars (2 Marks):
Isotopes: Atoms of the same element having the same atomic number (Z), but different mass numbers (A). E.g., Carbon-12 (126C) and Carbon-14 (146C).
Isobars: Atoms of different elements having different atomic numbers (Z), but the same mass number (A). E.g., Argon-40 (4018Ar) and Calcium-40 (4020Ca).
Applications (1 Mark - 0.5 Mark each):
Uranium-235 (235U) is used as fuel in nuclear power plants.
Cobalt-60 (60Co) is used in cancer radiotherapy / Iodine-131 (131I) is used to treat goitre.

SECTION D: Integrated Atomic Structure & Isotopic Calculation

Q9. Isotopic Calculation
Marking Scheme / Steps: (i) Average Atomic Mass Calculation (2 Marks):
Average Mass = (35 * 75/100) + (37 * 25/100) = (35 * 3/4) + (37 * 1/4) = (105 + 37) / 4 = 142 / 4 = 35.5 u.
(ii) Particles in 3717Cl (1.5 Marks - 0.5 Mark each):
Protons = 17, Electrons = 17, Neutrons = 37 - 17 = 20.
(iii) Electronic Configuration (1.5 Marks):
Configuration: K = 2, L = 8, M = 7.
Electron distribution: K shell contains 2 electrons, L shell contains 8, M shell contains 7.
(iv) Ion Formation Reason (1 Mark):
Gaining 1 electron requires much less energy to complete its octet (achieving a stable 8-electron outer shell) than losing 7 valence electrons.
