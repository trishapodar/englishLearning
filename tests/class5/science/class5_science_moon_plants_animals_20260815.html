Section B

Q5. Sound on the Moon
Model Answer & Marking: Reason: Sound waves require a material medium (like air/gas) to travel. Since the Moon has no atmosphere (vacuum), sound cannot travel through space, making direct speech impossible without radio communication.
Marking: [1 Mark] for mentioning "no atmosphere/vacuum", [1 Mark] for stating "sound requires a medium to travel".
Q6. Vegetative Propagation
Model Answer & Marking: (a) Definition: Vegetative propagation is a form of plant reproduction in which a new plant grows from a vegetative part of the parent plant such as roots, stems, or leaves (without seeds). [1 Mark]
(b) Examples:
From Leaves: Bryophyllum / Begonia. [0.5 Mark]
From Underground Stem: Potato (eyes/buds) / Ginger / Onion. [0.5 Mark]
Q7. Migration
Model Answer & Marking: (a) Definition of Migration: Migration is the seasonal mass movement of animals from one region to another in search of food, warmer weather, or suitable breeding grounds. [1 Mark]
(b) Examples (Any two): Arctic Tern, Siberian Crane, Monarch Butterfly, Salmon fish. [1 Mark] (0.5 Mark for each valid example).

Section C

Q8. Weight on Moon & Solar Eclipse
Model Answer & Marking: (a) Weight Calculation on Moon:
Weight on Moon = Weight on Earth × (1/6) [0.5 Mark]
Weight on Moon = 66 kg ÷ 6 = 11 kg [1 Mark]
Answer: 11 kg
(b) Solar Eclipse Alignment & Moon Phase:
Alignment: Sun → Moon → Earth (The Moon comes directly between the Sun and Earth). [1 Mark]
Moon Phase: It occurs on a New Moon day (Amavasya). [0.5 Mark]
Q9. Seed Germination and Dispersal
Model Answer & Marking: (a) Three Conditions for Germination:
Air (Oxygen): For respiration and energy release. [0.5 Mark]
Water (Moisture): To soften the seed coat and activate enzymes. [0.5 Mark]
Warmth (Sunlight/Proper Temperature): For cell division and growth. [0.5 Mark]
(b) Adaptations for Dispersal:
Dandelion (Wind): Seeds are very light and have tufts of fine, silky hair (parachutes) that enable them to float long distances in the air. [0.75 Mark]
Coconut (Water): Has a thick fibrous, spongy outer husk that traps air and allows the fruit to float on water across rivers and oceans. [0.75 Mark]

Section D

Q10. Case-Based Model Answers
Case-Based Model Answers: Part (a) Breathing Organs [1.5 Marks]:
(i) Fish: Gills [0.5 Mark]
(ii) Dolphin: Lungs (via blowhole on top of the head) [0.5 Mark]
(iii) Earthworm: Moist skin [0.5 Mark]

Part (b) Dolphin Breathing Mechanism [1.5 Marks]:
Dolphins are mammals and have lungs, not gills. [0.5 Mark]
They cannot absorb dissolved oxygen directly from water. They must rise to the surface to breathe atmospheric oxygen through their blowhole. [1 Mark]

Part (c) Protective Function of Shells [1 Mark]:
The hard shell serves as an outer shield (exoskeleton) that protects the animal's soft internal organs from physical injury and shields them from predators when threatened. [1 Mark]
