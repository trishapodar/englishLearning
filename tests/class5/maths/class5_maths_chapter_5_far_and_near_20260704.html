Identify the distance from Bhopal to the waterfall: 17 km 900 m.
Set up the subtraction: 48 km 700 m - 17 km 900 m
Since 700 m is less than 900 m, borrow 1 km (1000 m) from 48 km, leaving 47 km.
Add the borrowed 1000 m to 700 m to get 1700 m.
Subtract the metres: 1700 m - 900 m = 800 m.
Subtract the kilometres: 47 km - 17 km = 30 km.
The final distance is 30 km 800 m.
