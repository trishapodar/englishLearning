Q2.   Kitchen Floor Tiling   [1 Mark(s)]

Correct Answer: A. 396

Area of floor = 220 cm × 180 cm = 39,600 cm².
Area of one tile = 10 cm × 10 cm = 100 cm².
Number of tiles = 39,600 / 100 = 396.

Marking: 1 mark for correct option selection.
Accept: A / 396 / A. 396
Common Error: Dividing by 10 instead of 100 (area of tile), getting 3960.

Ref: NCERT Class 5 Mathematics, Ch. 1: The Fish Tale

Q3.   Rectangle from Wire   [1 Mark(s)]

Correct Answer: B. 9 cm

Perimeter = 30 cm. Perimeter = 2 × (length + width).
30 = 2 × (length + 6) ⇒ length + 6 = 15 ⇒ length = 9 cm.

Marking: 1 mark for correct option selection.
Accept: B / 9 cm / B. 9 cm
Common Error: Subtracting 6 directly from 30 (getting 24 cm) or dividing 30 by 2 and not subtracting 6.

Ref: NCERT Class 5 Mathematics, Ch. 1: The Fish Tale

SECTION B — 3 Mark(s) Each

Q11.   Latha's Change Combination   [3 Mark(s)]

1. The total cost of the pencil and pen is seven and a half rupees, which is ₹ 7.50. Latha gave ₹ 10. The balance money returned is: ₹ 10 - ₹ 7.50 = ₹ 2.50 (two and a half rupees).

One possible combination of coins to make ₹ 2.50 using half-rupee (₹ 0.50) and quarter-rupee (₹ 0.25) coins is:
→ 4 half-rupee coins (4 × ₹ 0.50 = ₹ 2.00)
→ 2 quarter-rupee coins (2 × ₹ 0.25 = ₹ 0.50)
Total = ₹ 2.00 + ₹ 0.50 = ₹ 2.50.

Marking: 1.5 marks for correct balance calculation (₹ 2.50). 1.5 marks for any valid coin combination.
Accept: Alternative valid combinations: e.g., 2 half-rupee and 6 quarter-rupee coins; 5 half-rupee coins.
Common Error: Incorrectly calculating change (e.g. ₹ 3.50) or providing a combination that does not sum to ₹ 2.50.

Ref: NCERT Class 5 Mathematics, Ch. 1: The Fish Tale

Q12.   Enlarged Square Plan Area   [3 Mark(s)]

1. The area of the original square is 2 cm × 2 cm = 4 cm².
2. The area of the enlarged square is 4 cm × 4 cm = 16 cm².
3. No, the area does not become two times bigger. It becomes four times bigger because 16 cm² / 4 cm² = 4. When the side length of a square is doubled, its area increases by 2 × 2 = 4 times.

Marking: 1 mark for original area (4 cm²). 1 mark for enlarged area (16 cm²). 1 mark for explanation of 4x growth instead of 2x.
Accept: Correct calculations with working shown.
Common Error: Thinking the area also doubles when side length is doubled (stating it becomes 8 cm² or saying 'yes' for part c).

Ref: NCERT Class 5 Mathematics, Ch. 8: Mapping Your Way

Q15.   Town Distance Scale Conversion   [3 Mark(s)]

To find the actual distance between the two towns, we multiply the distance measured on the map by the scale factor.

→ Distance on the map = 5 cm
→ Scale of the map: 1 cm = 10 km
→ Calculation: Actual distance = 5 × 10 km = 50 km

Therefore, the actual distance between Idlipur and Barfinagar is 50 km.

Marking: 1 mark for writing down given map distance and scale. 1 mark for setting up multiplication step (5 × 10). 1 mark for calculating correct final answer with units (50 km).
Accept: Correct final answer of 50 km with step.
Common Error: Adding instead of multiplying (e.g., 15 km) or using wrong units.

Ref: NCERT Class 5 Mathematics, Ch. 8: Mapping Your Way
