Section A

Q1. (D) 103 litres
Marking Scheme / Steps: Identify the initial quantity of fuel: 28 litres.
Identify the additional quantity of fuel filled: 75 litres.
Set up the addition: 28 + 75.
Add the digits in the ones place: 8 + 5 = 13 (write 3 in the ones place and carry over 1 to the tens place).
Add the digits in the tens place along with the carryover: 2 + 7 + 1 = 10.
The total sum is 103 litres.
Q2. (C) 34,357 runs
Marking Scheme / Steps: Understand that since Virat Kohli has 6,758 runs less than Sachin Tendulkar, Sachin Tendulkar must have scored more runs than Virat.
Set up the addition equation: Sachin's runs = Virat's runs + 6,758.
Substitute the values: 27,599 + 6,758.
Calculate the sum: 27,599 + 6,758 = 34,357 runs.
Q3. (C) ₹60,780
Marking Scheme / Steps: Align the digits of both numbers according to their place values: 21,880 and 38,900.
Add the digits starting from the Ones place:
Ones: 0 + 0 = 0
Tens: 8 + 0 = 8
Hundreds: 8 + 9 = 17 (write 7, carry over 1 to the Thousands place)
Thousands: 1 + 8 + 1 (carry) = 10 (write 0, carry over 1 to the Ten Thousands place)
Ten Thousands: 2 + 3 + 1 (carry) = 6
Combining these, the total sum is 21,880 + 38,900 = 60,780. Thus, the total amount spent is ₹60,780.
Q4. (C) 1 Ten and 3 Ones
Marking Scheme / Steps: Add the digits in the ones place: 8 + 5 = 13 ones.
Recall the standard place value regrouping rule from the text: 10 Ones = 1 Ten.
Express 13 ones as 10 ones + 3 ones.
Regroup the 10 ones as 1 ten, which gives 1 Ten and 3 Ones.
Q5. (C) Interchange 2 from Group A and 3 from Group B
Marking Scheme / Steps: Calculate the target sum for each group: Find the total sum of both groups, which is 19 + 21 = 40. Since we want both groups to have equal sums, the target sum for each group is 40 ÷ 2 = 20.
Determine the required change: Group A currently has a sum of 19, which is 1 less than the target sum of 20. Group B has a sum of 21, which is 1 more than 20.
Find the pair to interchange: We need to swap a number x from Group A with a number y from Group B such that y - x = 1.
Test the options: If we interchange 2 from Group A and 3 from Group B (since 3 - 2 = 1):
New Group A: 1, 3, 7, 9 with sum 1 + 3 + 7 + 9 = 20.
New Group B: 2, 4, 5, 9 with sum 2 + 4 + 5 + 9 = 20. Both sums are now equal.
Q6. (A) 38,450
Marking Scheme / Steps: Identify the lower limit, which is 38,125, and the upper limit, which is 38,600.
Compare each option to find a number x that satisfies the condition 38,125 < x < 38,600.
Check 38,450: Since 38,450 is greater than 38,125 and less than 38,600, it lies between them.
Verify that other options do not satisfy the condition: 38,105 is less than 38,125; 38,650 is greater than 38,600; and 37,950 is less than 38,125.
Q7. (B) Scale
Marking Scheme / Steps: Recall the basic components of a pictograph.
Identify that the representation of a specific quantity by a single symbol (such as 1 symbol = 3 items) is called the scale.
Q8. (C) ON, because 23 is an odd number.
Marking Scheme / Steps: Observe the pattern: The torch is ON on odd-numbered presses (1st, 3rd, etc.) and OFF on even-numbered presses (2nd, etc.).
Identify the nature of the number 23: Since 23 cannot be grouped into perfect pairs (or ends with 3), it is an odd number.
Relate the nature of the number to the state: Since 23 is an odd number, the torch will be ON.
Q9. (B) ₹920
Marking Scheme / Steps: Find the number of groups of 3 books Mala bought: 18 ÷ 3 = 6 groups.
Calculate the cost of these 6 groups: 6 × ₹150 = ₹900.
Add the remaining money to find the total money Mala had at the beginning: ₹900 + ₹20 = ₹920.
Q10. (A) 334
Marking Scheme / Steps: 1. The given equation is 666 + missing number = 1,000.
2. To find the missing number, subtract 666 from 1,000: 1,000 - 666.
3. Subtracting 666 from 1,000 gives 334.
4. Thus, the correct option is 334.

To find the sequence for the 10 slits, we apply the rule step-by-step:

The first slit is 1 under (which does not repeat): Under.
The remaining 9 slits (10 - 1 = 9) must follow the repeating pattern of 3 over, 3 under:
Slits 2, 3, and 4: Over, Over, Over
Slits 5, 6, and 7: Under, Under, Under
Slits 8, 9, and 10: Over, Over, Over

Thus, the complete sequence for the 10 slits is: Under, Over, Over, Over, Under, Under, Under, Over, Over, Over.
Marking Scheme / Steps: [1 Mark] Correctly identifying the first slit as 'Under' using the non-repeating rule.
[1 Mark] Correctly applying the repeating '3 over, 3 under' pattern for the next 9 slits (Slits 2 to 10).
[1 Mark] Writing the final correct 10-step sequence: Under, Over, Over, Over, Under, Under, Under, Over, Over, Over.
