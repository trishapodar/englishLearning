SECTION B — 2 Mark(s) Each

Q3.   Plastids in Plant Roots   [2 Mark(s)]

Rohit is correct. (1 Mark)

Reason: Chloroplasts contain chlorophyll required for photosynthesis, which requires sunlight. Since roots are underground and do not receive sunlight, they do not have chloroplasts (they typically contain leucoplasts for starch storage instead). (1 Mark)

Marking: 1 mark for identifying Rohit as correct. 1 mark for correct scientific reason.
Accept: Any correctly framed explanation referencing leucoplasts or lack of sunlight.
Common Error: Stating both are partially correct.

Ref: NCERT Class 9 Science, Ch: The Fundamental Unit of Life

Q4.   Cell Division Control Loss   [2 Mark(s)]

1. The cells have lost the mechanism of contact inhibition (the process where normal cells stop dividing when they touch neighboring cells). (1 Mark)

2. This uncontrolled, continuous division indicates the formation of a tumour, which is characteristic of cancer. (1 Mark)

Marking: 1 mark for naming 'contact inhibition'. 1 mark for identifying 'cancer' or 'tumour'.
Accept: Valid equivalents for cell cycle regulation loss.

SECTION C — 7 Mark(s) Each

Q5.   Golgi Apparatus Damage Assessment   [7 Mark(s)]

(a) Primary Function: The Golgi apparatus is responsible for the storage, modification, and packaging of products (like proteins and lipids) synthesized by the Endoplasmic Reticulum (ER) into vesicles. (2 Marks)

(b) Consequences of Damage (1 Mark for each valid point, max 3):
1. Proteins and lipids synthesized by the ER will not be modified or packaged, halting intracellular transport.
2. The cell will be unable to secrete necessary substances (like enzymes or hormones) to the outside environment.
3. The formation of new lysosomes will stop, preventing the cell from digesting waste materials or foreign pathogens.
4. Complex sugars will not be made from simple sugars.

(c) Bacterial Cell Response: No, the bacterial cell would not suffer the same specific damage. (1 Mark)
Reason: Bacteria are prokaryotes, and prokaryotic cells lack membrane-bound organelles, including the Golgi Apparatus. Therefore, there is no Golgi apparatus to destroy. (1 Mark)

Marking: (a) 2 marks for stating storage, modification, and packaging. (b) 3 marks for any 3 valid physiological consequences. (c) 1 mark for stating 'No' and 1 mark for stating prokaryotes lack membrane-bound organelles.
Accept: Valid biological explanations covering secretion, lysosome formation, and prokaryotic structure.
Common Error: Failing to link Golgi function with lysosome formation.

Ref: NCERT Class 9 Science, Ch: The Fundamental Unit of Life
