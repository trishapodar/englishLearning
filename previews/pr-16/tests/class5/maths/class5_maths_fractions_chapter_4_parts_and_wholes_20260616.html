Q13.   Completing the Whole from Fractional Parts   [3 Mark(s)]

1. Since 3 petals represent 3/5 of the flower, 1/5 of the flower is represented by 1 petal (3 ÷ 3 = 1).

2. The complete flower represents the whole, which is 5/5. Thus, the total number of petals is 1 × 5 = 5 petals.

3. The number of additional petals to be drawn is 5 - 3 = 2 petals.

Marking: Identify the unit fraction: State that if 3 petals equal 3/5, then 1/5 of the flower is equal to 1 petal. [1 Mark] Determine the whole: Calculate the total number of petals for the complete flower (5/5) as 1 × 5 = 5 petals. [1 Mark] Find the remaining parts: Subtract the existing petals from the total (5 - 3 = 2 petals) to find how many more petals need to be drawn. [1 Mark]
Accept: Any valid step-by-step reasoning arriving at 5 total and 2 remaining.
Common Error: None specified.

Ref: Chapter 4

Q14.   Cost Calculation with Mixed Fractions   [3 Mark(s)]

The cost of 2 1/4 kg of carrots is Rs. 36.

Marking: Identify the price per kg (Rs. 16) and convert the mixed fraction of weight to an improper fraction: 2 1/4 = (2 × 4) + 1/4 = 9/4 kg. [1 Mark] Multiply the weight by the price per kg to set up the equation: Total Cost = 9/4 × 16. [1 Mark] Simplify the expression to calculate the final cost: 9 × 4 = Rs. 36. [1 Mark]
Accept: Correct calculation.
Common Error: None specified.

Ref: Chapter 4

Q15.   Dividing Shapes into Equal Parts   [3 Mark(s)]

(a) Each part represents 1/6 of its respective sheet.

(b) 1/6 of the green sheet is bigger than 1/6 of the blue sheet. This is because the green sheet is larger in size than the blue sheet. When the whole is larger, its equal parts (fractions) are also larger compared to the same fraction of a smaller whole.

Marking: Award 1 mark for correctly identifying the fraction as 1/6 for each part of both sheets. Award 1 mark for stating that 1/6 of the green sheet is bigger than 1/6 of the blue sheet. Award 1 mark for explaining that the size of a fraction depends on the size of the whole (larger whole means larger fractional part).
Accept: Correct explanation.
