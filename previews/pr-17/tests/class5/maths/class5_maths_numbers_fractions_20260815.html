Section A

Q1. (b) 10 Lakhs
Explanation: 1 Million = 1,000,000. In the Indian system, 1,000,000 is written as 10,00,000 (10 Lakhs).
Marking Scheme: [1 Mark] for option (b).
Q2. (b) 367
Explanation: Dividend = (Divisor × Quotient) + Remainder = (15 × 24) + 7 = 360 + 7 = 367.
Marking Scheme: [1 Mark] for option (b).
Q3. (c) 435
Explanation: A number divisible by 5 must end in 0 or 5. A number divisible by 3 must have the sum of its digits divisible by 3. For 435: ends in 5, and 4 + 3 + 5 = 12 (divisible by 3).
Marking Scheme: [1 Mark] for option (c).
Q4. (c) 12/20
Explanation: Multiplying both numerator and denominator by 4: (3 × 4) / (5 × 4) = 12/20.
Marking Scheme: [1 Mark] for option (c).

Section B

Q5. Place Value and Face Value
Model Answer & Step-by-Step Marks: (i) Place Value of 7: Digit 7 is in the Ten-Thousands place = 70,000 [0.5 Mark]
(ii) Face Value of 7: 7 [0.5 Mark]
(iii) Difference: 70,000 - 7 = 69,993 [1 Mark]
Q6. HCF and LCM of 18 and 24
Model Answer & Step-by-Step Marks: Prime Factorization: 18 = 2 × 3 × 3 [0.5 Mark] | 24 = 2 × 2 × 2 × 3 [0.5 Mark]
HCF Calculation: Common prime factors = 2 × 3 = 6 [0.5 Mark]
LCM Calculation: Highest powers = 2 × 2 × 2 × 3 × 3 = 8 × 9 = 72 [0.5 Mark]
Q7. Fraction Addition
Model Answer & Step-by-Step Marks: Step 1: Convert to improper fractions: 2 1/4 = (2 × 4 + 1) / 4 = 9/4 [0.5 Mark] | 1 2/3 = (1 × 3 + 2) / 3 = 5/3
Step 2: Find LCM of denominators (4 and 3): LCM = 12. 9/4 = (9 × 3) / (4 × 3) = 27/12. 5/3 = (5 × 4) / (3 × 4) = 20/12 [0.5 Mark]
Step 3: Add like fractions: 27/12 + 20/12 = 47/12 [0.5 Mark]
Step 4: Convert to mixed fraction: 47 ÷ 12 = 3 with remainder 11 → 3 11/12 [0.5 Mark]

Section C

Q8. Multiplication and Division Word Problem
Model Answer & Step-by-Step Marks: (a) Milk packed in March (31 days):
Daily production = 375 liters. Total milk = 375 × 31. 375 × 1 = 375, 375 × 30 = 11,250. 11,250 + 375 = 11,625 liters. Answer: 11,625 liters [1.5 Marks]
(b) Number of cans required:
Total milk = 1,875 liters. Capacity per can = 25 liters. Number of cans = 1,875 ÷ 25 = 75. Answer: 75 cans [1.5 Marks]
Q9. Fraction Word Problem
Model Answer & Step-by-Step Marks: (a) Total ribbon used:
Used for card = 1 1/2 = 3/2 meters. Used for gift = 2 1/4 = 9/4 meters.
Total used = 3/2 + 9/4. Convert to like denominator: (3 × 2) / (2 × 2) = 6/4. 6/4 + 9/4 = 15/4 meters = 3 3/4 meters.
Answer: 15/4 meters (or 3 3/4 meters) [1.5 Marks]
(b) Ribbon remaining:
Total ribbon = 5 meters = 20/4 meters.
Remaining ribbon = 5 - 15/4 = 20/4 - 15/4 = 5/4 meters.
Convert to mixed fraction: 5/4 = 1 1/4 meters.
Answer: 1 1/4 meters (or 1.25 meters) [1.5 Marks]

Section D

Q10. Case-Based Model Answers
Case-Based Model Answers: Part (a) Maximum Students Per Group (HCF) [1.5 Marks]:
Prime factors of 120: 2 × 2 × 2 × 3 × 5. Prime factors of 84: 2 × 2 × 3 × 7 [0.5 Mark]. Common factors: 2 × 2 × 3 = 12. Answer: The maximum number of students in each group is 12 students. [1 Mark]

Part (b) Next Simultaneous Beep Time (LCM) [1.5 Marks]:
Prime factors of 15: 3 × 5. Prime factors of 20: 2 × 2 × 5 [0.5 Mark]. LCM = 2 × 2 × 3 × 5 = 60 minutes = 1 hour [0.5 Mark]. Next beep time = 9:00 a.m. + 1 hour = 10:00 a.m. Answer: 10:00 a.m. [0.5 Mark]

Part (c) Fraction of Pizza Left [1 Mark]:
Fraction eaten by Aman = 3/8. Fraction eaten by Priya = 2/8. Total fraction eaten = 3/8 + 2/8 = 5/8 [0.5 Mark]. Fraction left = 1 - 5/8 = 8/8 - 5/8 = 3/8. Answer: 3/8 of the pizza [0.5 Mark]
