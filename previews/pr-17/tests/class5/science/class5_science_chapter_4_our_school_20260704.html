Q1.
The Swachh Bharat Swachh Vidyalaya (Clean India Clean Schools) is an initiative started by the Government of India under the Swachh Bharat Mission. Its main objective is to ensure that every child has the right to study in a clean, safe, and healthy environment.

The four key focus areas of this initiative are:

Clean toilets: Ensuring clean and usable sanitation facilities.
Safe drinking water: Providing healthy and clean water for all.
Proper waste management: Keeping the school premises free of litter.
Hygiene education: Teaching students healthy habits and cleanliness.
Marking Scheme / Steps: State the definition and main objective of the Swachh Bharat Swachh Vidyalaya initiative (1 mark).
Identify and explain any two focus areas, such as clean toilets and safe drinking water (1 mark).

Separating waste into dry and wet categories is important because it helps in managing waste better and keeping our surroundings clean. This practice makes the work of sanitation workers much safer and easier, as they do not have to sort through mixed, dirty waste, protecting them from health hazards. Additionally, keeping recyclable items like newspapers separate helps traditional recyclers collect and reuse them easily, promoting the dignity of their labour.
Marking Scheme / Steps: Importance of Segregation (1 Mark): Explaining that separating waste into dry and wet categories helps in better waste management and keeps our surroundings clean.
Safety of Sanitation Workers (1 Mark): Explaining that segregation makes the work of sanitation workers safer and easier by reducing the need to handle mixed, unhygienic waste.
Role of Recyclers (1 Mark): Explaining that keeping recyclable items separate allows traditional recyclers to collect and reuse them easily.
