Trees play an important role in slowing down rain and helping the soil soak up water. When there are no trees, rainwater runs off the land much faster, which causes flooding. Floods affect our daily lives in several ways, such as damaging homes (forcing people and animals to leave to stay safe) and destroying crops, which badly affects our food supply.
Marking Scheme / Steps: Cause of Flooding (1 Mark): Explain that trees slow down rain and help soil soak up water. Without trees, water runs off the land faster, causing floods.
First Effect (1 Mark): Mention that floods damage homes, forcing people and animals to leave their homes to stay safe.
Second Effect (1 Mark): Mention any other valid effect from the text, such as lost crops affecting food supply, dirty water spreading diseases, or broken roads affecting travel.

Q3.
Fertilisers are highly soluble in water, so they dissolve and remain invisible, making the river look clean. However, these dissolved chemicals act as nutrients that make water plants grow extremely fast. This rapid growth forms a 'green blanket' over the water surface. This blanket chokes the river by blocking sunlight and air, making it very difficult for the fish inside to breathe and survive.
Marking Scheme / Steps: [1 Mark] Explain that fertilisers dissolve in water (solubility) and are invisible, but promote rapid plant growth.
[1 Mark] Explain that this rapid growth of plants covers the river surface like a 'green blanket'.
[1 Mark] Analyze the impact on aquatic life, stating that it chokes the river and makes it hard for fish to breathe.
Q4.
Trees play a vital role in preventing floods because they slow down rain and help the soil soak up water. When there are no trees, rainwater runs off the land much faster, causing floods. Floods severely affect our lives by damaging homes (leaving people without shelter) and breaking roads, which disrupts travel and transport. They also destroy crops, leading to a shortage in the food supply, and cause the spread of diseases.
Marking Scheme / Steps: Cause of Flooding (1 Mark): Explain that trees slow down rain and help soil soak up water. Without trees, water runs off the land faster, causing floods.
Effect 1 (1 Mark): Identify one valid effect of floods on daily life or infrastructure (e.g., damaged homes affecting shelter, broken roads disrupting transport, or lost crops affecting food supply).
Effect 2 (1 Mark): Identify a second valid effect of floods (e.g., dirty water/spread of diseases affecting health, power outages, or schools shutting down).
