Model Answer:
The Dignity of Labour and Nation-Building
The poem "Gifts of Grace: Honouring Our Vocations" presents a vibrant portrait of a thriving nation powered by the dedicated labour of its artisans, technicians, and manual workers. A progressive society does not depend solely on white-collar professions; it stands firmly on the foundation laid by carpenters, masons, electricians, farmers, and health workers.

True dignity of labour lies in recognizing that every vocation requires distinct skill, creativity, and perseverance. When a shoemaker crafts durable footwear or an electrician wires a home safely, they contribute directly to societal well-being. Looking down upon manual trades creates artificial social divides.

By celebrating the phrase "I hear Bharat celebrating," the poem urges us to shed vocational prejudices. Instilling respect for all trades fosters social harmony and inspires the next generation to pursue vocational skills with pride and excellence.
